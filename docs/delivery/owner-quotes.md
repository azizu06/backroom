## Short interview guide

1. What finance work did you or your office manager handle this week? Ask for one concrete late-invoice, supplier-payment or vendor-change example.
2. What happened, how did you handle it, and what time or work did it cost? Record their account without supplying an answer.
3. Which parts could an assistant do, and which would still need you? What evidence would you need before approving an exception?
4. Read back the proposed excerpt. Confirm the exact wording and attribution, where it may appear, and whether recording/publication is permitted. Participation does not imply product endorsement or a paying customer.
